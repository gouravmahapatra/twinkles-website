Profile

A designer with both visual sensitivity and real delivery experience.

Extracted from the portfolio, Namrata Mahapatra’s profile is grounded in concept development, mood boards, visual story, material selection, client communication, and project management from concept to completion. That combination matters because the strongest interiors are not just beautiful, they are also clearly communicated, buildable, and aligned with the client’s lifestyle or business goals.

Her work ranges from calm, minimal residences and luxury villas to hospitality concepts, workspaces, and institutional projects. The portfolio also shows an ability to build highly detailed, room-by-room presentation sets, making it easy for clients to understand the design direction before execution begins.
